About TrafficSign.AI

An end-to-end intelligent road safety platform providing instant traffic sign classification, context awareness, and driver guidance via Deep Convolutional Neural Networks.

CNN Model Architecture Pipeline

The system employs a multi-stage Deep Convolutional Neural Network designed for spatial feature invariance, lighting resilience, and high-speed CPU/GPU inference.

Stage 1: Input & Augmentation

64×64×3 RGB normalization, RandomRotation (±8°), RandomZoom (±10%), RandomTranslation (±8%).

Stage 2: Feature Hierarchy

3 Sequential Conv Blocks: 32 → 64 → 128 filters with BatchNormalization, ReLU, 2×2 MaxPool, and Dropout (0.25–0.35).

Stage 3: Dense Classification

Flatten layer feeding into 256-unit Dense layer with ReLU, BatchNorm, 50% Dropout, and 20-way Softmax output.
